Slot RG35XX SP 0.2.0 -- eight-system single-card image

For Anbernic RG35XX SP only. Do not use on RG SP, RG34XX SP or G350.
Experimental hardware port: boot, sound, hinge and game speed need checking
on your new handheld. GB/GBC/GBA/SNES/PS1/N64/DC/PSP emulators are included.
No games or proprietary BIOS files are included.

1. Extract slot-rg35xxsp-0.2.0.img.zip. Flash its .img to a 16 GB or larger
   microSD using your usual imager (for example Rufus or balenaEtcher).
   Flashing replaces the contents of the selected card. Keep your G350
   card intact; use a different card for the SP.
2. Put that card in TF1; leave TF2 empty. Boot with the charger disconnected
   and the device charged. Leave the lid open during initial setup.
   FIRST BOOT expands and reformats the data partition, then installs Slot.
   Wait for Slot's clock/setup screen BEFORE copying any games.
3. Set the clock, shut down through Slot's Power menu, move the card to the
   computer, and use the large BASEOS data partition. Windows may also
   offer to format small Linux partitions: cancel those prompts.
4. Copy your existing G350 Slot/Games and Slot/Labels folders to Games and
   Labels at the ROOT of BASEOS. Copy BIOS and Saves similarly. Keep the
   SP's new System folder. Do not copy G350 SYSTEM, SYSTEM.md5, System,
   Config/slot.state or Linux/boot partitions onto this SP card.
5. Safely eject and boot. Hold Menu alone about one second to return to
   Slot. The upstream one-cart mode prevents ejecting the only game;
   have at least two games on the card to use the shelf normally.

Game folders (at BASEOS root):
  Games/GB     .gb / .gbc
  Games/GBC    .gbc / .gb
  Games/GBA    .gba
  Games/SNES   .sfc / .smc
  Games/PS1    .chd / .cue (keep .bin tracks) / .m3u / .pbp
  Games/N64    .z64 / .n64 / .v64
  Games/DC     .chd / .gdi (keep tracks) / .cdi / .cue / .m3u
  Games/PSP    .iso / .cso / .pbp / .chd
Extract .zip/.7z ROMs first. A multidisc playlist must use relative paths.

Artwork: copy your existing Labels/<SYSTEM> folders. For new artwork open
Tools/Slot-Auto-Artwork-Studio-v4.html on the computer in Chrome/Edge and
choose BASEOS itself (the folder containing Games, Labels and System).
It supports SNES/PS1/N64/DC/PSP; GB/GBC/GBA use the original Cart Studio.

Save/load shortcuts:
  PS1/SNES/N64/DC/PSP: Menu + X saves, Menu + Y loads.
  GB/GBC/GBA: Select + R1 saves, Select + L1 loads (tap second button
  within 0.6 seconds). L2 rewinds, R2 fast-forwards; X/Y are turbo A/B.
  Double-tap Menu: saved-state picture browser. Left/Right chooses,
  A loads, B returns, X undoes the last action, Y deletes that state.
  PS1 only: Menu + R2 cycles the discs of an .m3u playlist.
  Power tap: sleep/wake. Power hold ~1 second: Restart / Power off menu.
  Volume +/-: volume. Both together: mute/unmute.
  Shelf: Left/Right games, Up/Down letters, L1/R1 systems, A starts.
  Hold A ~0.5 seconds on shelf to start clean without loading a resume.

SP analog controls (there are no physical sticks):
  N64 starts with D-pad = left analog stick.
  DC/PSP start with D-pad = digital directional buttons.
  Menu + Select switches between digital D-pad and analog stick while
  playing N64/DC/PSP. The toast confirms the new mode. It resets on launch.
  N64: A=A, B=B, L2=Z, L1=L, R1=R, Start=Start;
  X=C-up, Y=C-left, R2=C-right, Select=C-down.
  PSP face positions match PlayStation: B=cross, A=circle, X=triangle,
  Y=square, L1=L, R1=R, Start=Start, Select=Select.
  D-pad analog mode gives full directional deflection; fine analog
  steering and simultaneous analog + digital directions are limited.

Performance: includes the accepted G350 cores/settings, native DC 320x240,
PSP 480x272, N64 320x240, JIT/dynarec and bounded frameskip. The SP uses its
own H700 kernel/Mali driver/audio support, with highest stock clocks.
DC/PSP/N64 speed depends on the game. Full-speed Tekken 6 is not guaranteed.
This is a port, not a measured benchmark on the RG35XX SP yet.

Normal in-game saves should transfer. Keep original save-state backups;
states are core/version dependent and may not be portable across devices.
Dreamcast VMUs: Saves/DC. PSP saves: Saves/PSP/MemoryStick/PSP/SAVEDATA.
PS1 and Dreamcast have HLE BIOS fallbacks. If needed, copy your own BIOS
to BIOS/ (PS1) and BIOS/dc/ (Dreamcast). PPSSPP assets are preinstalled.

If first boot fails, inspect slot-install.log and baseos-boot.log on BASEOS.
If Slot or a game fails, inspect slot.log (previous launch: slot.log.1).
Use this same image again for a fresh flash; G350 updates are incompatible.
Generic upstream BaseOS updates do not include this custom installer.
