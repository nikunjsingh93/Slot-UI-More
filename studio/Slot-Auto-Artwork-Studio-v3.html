Choose Slot once, preview the results, then save ready labels together. Internet is needed for artwork. ROM contents are never uploaded or read for matching; small PS1 playlists are read locally to group discs. Existing labels stay intact unless you enable replacement.

The default style prefers original disc printing and cartridge labels. PS1 scans come from PSX1G1B1C1D; SNES and N64 scans come from LaunchBox Games Database, delivered by wsrv.nl. If no usable scan exists, Libretro logos are used, then covers. Each preview identifies its source and any fallback.

Cartridge scans are cropped automatically. Use “Adjust scan crop” only when a scan needs alignment; the full scan appears there. PS1 playlists use Disc 1 art; separately listed Disc 2/3 files keep their disc number. Matching uses filenames and may need correction for unusual names. Catalog snapshot: 2026-10-08. SNES labels: 224×139; PS1: 312×312; N64: 180×190. Use Slot G350 0.1.3 for N64 and complete printed-disc previews. Restart Slot after saving.
