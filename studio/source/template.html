One studio for all eight systems. You can also choose a BASEOS card folder containing Games and Labels.

Choose Slot once, preview the results, then save ready labels together. Internet is needed for artwork. ROM contents are never uploaded or read for matching; small PS1/DC playlists are read locally to group discs. Existing labels stay intact unless you enable replacement.

The default style prefers original disc printing and cartridge labels. PS1 scans come from PSX1G1B1C1D; GB, GBC, GBA, SNES, N64, Dreamcast and PSP scans come from LaunchBox Games Database, delivered by wsrv.nl. If no usable scan exists, Libretro logos are used, then covers. Each preview identifies its source and any fallback.

Cartridge scans are cropped automatically. Disc scans keep their proportions. PS1/DC keep spindle holes; PSP preserves the printed UMD hub. Use “Adjust scan crop” only when a scan needs alignment; the full scan appears there. PS1 playlists use Disc 1 art; DC playlists are grouped; separately listed Disc 2/3 files keep their disc number. Matching uses filenames and may need correction for unusual names. Original catalog snapshot: __CATALOG_DATE__; GB/GBC/GBA added 2026-10-19. GB/GBC labels: 220×195; GBA: 266×138; SNES labels: 224×139; PS1: 312×312; N64: 180×190; Dreamcast and PSP disc labels: 312×312. PSP previews include the UMD shell. Dreamcast and PSP labels work with Slot G350 0.1.9.4. Restart Slot after saving.
